# PRODUCT.md — Asperus Cast

## Mission
Transform any screen with a modern web browser (such as Smart TVs, Android TVs, Apple TVs, or secondary monitors) into an instant photo and video showcase, effortlessly managed in real-time from a mobile smartphone without requiring accounts, native app store installations, or local server setups.

## Target Audience
- Family & Gatherings: Users who want to share photos/videos from their phone gallery directly on a large TV in seconds.
- Events & Showrooms: Displays, shops, or parties running a continuous, elegant slideshow loop with custom timings.

## Core Pillars
1. **Zero Friction Onboarding**: Instant pairing via TV QR code camera scan or 4-letter room code.
2. **Serverless & Resilient Sync**: Real-time room synchronization through Cloudflare Edge Workers and cloud asset storage (ImgBB/Catbox), working even on restrictive Smart TV web engines.
3. **Impeccable Visual Craft**: Metallic silver aesthetic, high contrast, clean typography (Syne, Space Grotesk, Outfit), and tactile controls designed for mobile interaction.
